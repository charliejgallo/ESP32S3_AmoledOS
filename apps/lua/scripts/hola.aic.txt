/* The icon of hola.lua: what the script itself draws, which is a cyan disc
 * that breathes. Two rings around it at falling opacity read as the pulse.
 *
 *     python3 tools/aic.py asm apps/lua/scripts/hola.aic.txt
 *
 * AIC_RING is centred by construction -it takes a diameter and a border and
 * nothing else- so the three shapes share a centre without any arithmetic.
 * The borders are AIC_DIV(28), the hairline the hand-drawn icons used: no
 * percentage reproduces a one-pixel line at 66, 74 and 82 px alike.
 *
 * The cyan is the literal the script passes to aos.disc(), not a palette
 * entry, so the two stay the same colour if either is edited.
 */

AIC_RING(78, AIC_DIV(28), AIC_C_LIT(0x00E5FF), 70),
AIC_RING(56, AIC_DIV(28), AIC_C_LIT(0x00E5FF), 140),
AIC_RECT(AIC_CENTER, 0, 0, 32, 32, AIC_CIRCLE, AIC_C_LIT(0x00E5FF), 255)
